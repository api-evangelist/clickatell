#  Augment | The Secondary Marketplace for Private Stock
> Augment is a FINRA-registered marketplace that allows accredited investors to buy and sell shares in over 300 pre-IPO companies, providing a seamless platform for secondary market transactions with low investment minimums starting at $10K.
